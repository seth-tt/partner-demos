More revenue per visit from OfferUp Services

    The three options side by side

    OfferUp already sends Thumbtack requests through Find Pros and Requests. A and C raise what those requests earn by cutting the pros contacted per request and the replies that go unread. B adds a new kind of revenue: a paid, booked job to one pro.
